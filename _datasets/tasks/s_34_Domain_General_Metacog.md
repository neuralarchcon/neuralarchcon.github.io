#### Detailed description

All tasks are two-alternative forced-choice (2AFC) tasks. After each decision, participants have to rate their confidence on the previous decision on a scale from 1 to 6 (1: 50% = guessing, 2: 60%, 3: 70%, 4: 80%, 5: 90%, 6: 100%). Task order is randomised. There is no time limit for providing either the decision or the confidence judgment.

The episodic-memory task is a paired associative-learning task. During the encoding phase, participants are presented with unrelated pairs of words for 2,500 ms. During the test phase, they have to decide which one of the two presented words was associated with the cue in the encoding phase. In the semantic-memory task, participants have to select the correct response on general-knowledge questions about art, cinema, sport, science, history, and geography (e.g., "What does the A in DNA stand for?"). In the visual perception task, participants are presented with 2 circles containing dots for 700 ms. Then, they have to select which one of the two circles contained more dots. In the executive function task, participants are presented with a letter-number sequence of five symbols for 1,000 ms (e.g., 7A5N2). After the presentation, they have to choose which one of the two presented responses corresponded to the sum of all numbers and letters (e.g., 14AN in the example above).

#### Number of participants (raw)

Aarhus: 253

#### Conditions and number of trials

Total of 480 trials (120 per task).

#### Behavioural Measures

For each task:

* Participant decision (key "S" or "L")
* Reaction time for decision (in milliseconds)
* Correct response ("S" or "L")
* Confidence (6-point scale)
* Reaction time for confidence (in milliseconds)
